Projects

CSI205TC2026

Video streaming over TCP and UDP

Built a Python socket streaming system in both flavours, split JPEG frames into MTU-sized chunks for UDP, and captured the traffic to compare delivery ratio, latency and jitter under paced and unpaced sending.

Result: TCP delivered 160/160 frames in every scenario; unpaced UDP lost 90.6% of frames at the receiver socket buffer.

CSI205TC2026

Hadoop vs Spark word count benchmark

Wrote the same job twice — a Java MapReduce program with a combiner and a PySpark RDD pipeline — then ran both three times over a 20 MB text corpus and verified that the counts matched exactly.

Result: 3.69 M words, 75,985 distinct; Spark finished the job in 2.78 s against 5.02 s for Hadoop.

Coursework2026

Income prediction with Spark MLlib

Full ML pipeline on the UCI Adult dataset: missing-value handling, string indexing, one-hot encoding, scaling, hyper-parameter search, and evaluation of logistic regression against a random forest.

Result: AUC 0.905 (LR) and 0.904 (RF) on the official test split.
